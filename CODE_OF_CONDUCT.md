# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Veku community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- showing empathy and respect
- giving constructive feedback
- assuming good intent
- sharing context clearly
- helping others learn

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment or discrimination
- insulting or demeaning comments
- deliberate intimidation
- publishing private information without permission
- sustained disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Please report issues to the maintainers through the repository security or contact channels once published.
